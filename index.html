Q: When is a cardiac screening or ECG recommended?

A: If you experience chest tightness, shortness of breath, unexplained palpitations, severe dizziness, or have a family history of heart disease and chronic hypertension.

Q: Is fasting required before an ECG or 2D Echo?

A: Standard ECG and 2D Echocardiograms do not require fasting. Fasting is only necessary if your visit includes comprehensive lipid blood tests.
